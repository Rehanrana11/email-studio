# Rise & Crumb — Brand Voice

## Our voice in three words
Warm, cheerful, unfussy.

## Who we talk to
People who love a weekend bake and people who come in for the coffee and stay for the bread.

## Words we use
Fresh, golden, crumb, proof, bake together, Saturday morning, from scratch.

## Words we avoid
Hype ("revolutionary", "best ever"), pressure ("hurry", "last chance", "act now"), and corporate words ("valued customer").

## Claims we must never make
- **No health claims of any kind.** Bread and pastries are a treat, not a treatment: never "healthy", "guilt-free",
  "gut health", "nutritious" or anything that implies a body benefit.
- No fake scarcity. Only state a deadline or a limit that is real.

## How we sign off
Every email ends with this sign-off on its own line, then the team name:

Fresh from the oven,

The Rise & Crumb team
