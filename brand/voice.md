# Clove & Copper — Brand Voice

> Clove & Copper Spice Co. is a **made-up** small-batch spice shop. Its founder, *Rafi*, is a made-up persona.
> The machine-checkable version of these rules lives in `studio.config.json`. If you change one, change both.

## Who we are (personality)
**Warm, generous, a little cheeky.** Like a friend leaning over the stove saying, "Here, taste this."
A copper pot on the stove is our picture of home: well-used, honest, never fussy.

- Warm, not gushing
- Cheeky, not silly
- Knows spice, never snobby about it
- Practical. We help a busy person put a good dinner on the table tonight.

## Who we talk to
Home cooks aged **25–50** who are stuck cooking the same five dinners.
They want to try something new without hunting through ten shops, and cooking is a small daily
pleasure for them, not a performance.

They are busy, curious and a bit tired by 6pm. Talk to **one** person, not a crowd.

## How we sound
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "you" and "we", short sentences | "customers", "users", corporate "Dear valued..." |
| Sensory words: toast, bloom, sizzle, smoky, warm, bright, fragrant | Hype words: revolutionary, game-changer, unbelievable, insane, best ever |
| One gentle joke or pun per email, at most | A pun in every line |
| Specific details (origin, what to cook it with, how much to use) | Vague claims ("the best spices ever") |
| Calm invitations ("Get the pan warm") | Pressure ("ACT NOW", "LAST CHANCE!!!") |

### Words we like
toast, bloom, sizzle, small-batch, pantry, weeknight, a pinch, "get the pan warm"

### Words we avoid
detox, cure, heal, boost, immunity, anti-inflammatory, superfood, miracle, hurry, don't miss out, guaranteed

## Formatting
- Subject lines: sentence case, **under 45 characters**, max **one** emoji (🌶️ or 🍳), never ALL CAPS.
- Preview text: 40–90 characters, adds to the subject and doesn't repeat it.
- Body: short paragraphs (1–3 lines), about **150 words** for the main message.
- **One** clear CTA per email, as a button-style line: `[Join The Spice Circle →]`.
- One "!" per email at most.

## Sender, sign-off and footer
- From name: **Rafi at Clove & Copper**
- Sign-off: `Warmly, Rafi & the Clove & Copper kitchen 🌶️`
- Every email ends with this footer (US CAN-SPAM needs a working unsubscribe and a postal address in every commercial email; in this lab both are placeholders):
  - `You're getting this because you signed up at Clove & Copper. Unsubscribe any time: {{unsubscribe_url}}`
  - `Clove & Copper Spice Co. (a made-up practice brand) · PO Box 000, Example City, NY 00000`

## Never say (hard rules)
- **No health claims.** Spices are food. Turmeric does not "fight inflammation", ginger does not "boost immunity",
  nothing "detoxes" or "burns fat". (In the US these are FTC/FDA problems, not just style problems.)
- **No fake urgency** or fake scarcity. The only deadline is the real one in the plan.
- **No misleading subjects** ("Re:", "Fwd:", "Your order" when there is no order).
- No mocking other brands, cuisines or anyone's cooking. Everyone is welcome at the stove.
- No made-up reviews or testimonials presented as real.

## Quick test before shipping
1. Would a friend actually say this out loud in a kitchen?
2. Is there exactly one CTA?
3. Any banned word or health claim? → fix it.
4. Subject under 45 characters, no ALL CAPS?
5. Does the offer match the plan exactly ($17 founding, $22 normal, deadline Friday 18 December)?
